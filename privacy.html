Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

ABC Trails does not collect any data. The developer, Vladyslav Shchuka, receives nothing from the app: no names, no usage numbers, no device identifiers, no crash reports.

What the app does not have

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs. All the code in the app is ours.
  • No network connection. The app makes none of its own and works the same in airplane mode. Purchases are checked with the App Store by iOS itself, not by code of ours.

What stays on the device

To work as a tracing app, ABC Trails remembers a few things for each child on the iPhone or iPad:

  • the child's first name, but only if a grown-up types one in for the My Name island;
  • the animal badge the child picked;
  • settings such as writing style, writing hand and sound;
  • progress, finished letters and collected stickers.

This is kept in the app's own storage on the device and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. To delete the information about one child and keep the app, remove that child in the ranger's log (the grown-ups section behind the check). If your device is backed up by iCloud or to a computer, the app's storage can be part of that backup. That is a choice you and Apple make in your device settings, and we have no access to it.

Children

ABC Trails is made for children aged 3 to 6 and is in the Kids category of the App Store. We do not collect personal information from anyone, children included, which is the standard the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) asks for. There is nothing to share, sell or use for advertising, and no behavioral advertising of any kind.

Everything that leaves the child's screen, which means buying and every link to a website, sits in the grown-ups section. A child reaches it only by passing a check that is written in words and needs reading.

Purchases

The one purchase, ABC Trails Full, is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, no Apple Account information and no record of who bought it.

If you write to us

An email to us shows us your address and what you wrote. We use it to reply and for nothing else, and we do not pass it on.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new text goes on this page with a new date. Questions about it can go to shchukavlad4@gmail.com.
