Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

ABC Trails does not collect personal information from anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no network requests made by the app.

What stays on your device

Progress, stickers, settings (writing style, hand, sound) and the optional child's name are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children's privacy

ABC Trails is made for children aged 3 to 6 and is offered in the Kids Category of the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising.

  • The child's name, if a grown-up enters it for the My Name island, is kept on the device and never leaves it.
  • No third party receives any information from the app. The app contains no ads, analytics or other third-party code.
  • Purchases and all links to outside websites are placed in the grown-ups section behind a parental gate, so a child cannot reach them by accident.
  • To delete your child's data, delete the app. That removes everything it stores. Reset progress (Advanced, in the grown-ups section) clears progress and stickers. You can also clear the child's name in the My Name island field, in the same section.

Purchases

ABC Trails Full is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase is active.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and message and use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact
